Cheeky Koko Privacy Policy

Effective 19 September 2026 · Applies to the Android game Cheeky Koko (com.vyom.cheekykoko)

Cheeky Koko is a platformer game made by Vyom Global (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game handles, who receives it, and what you can do about it. If you have any question, write to vyomglobaltech@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your progress, settings and purchases are saved on your phone. We cannot see them.
  • The game uses Google services to handle purchases, measure how the game is played and show ads. Those services receive information about your device and how you use the game, as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored only on your device

The game saves your level progress, stars, settings, chosen character skin, boosters, daily-bonus status and the items you have bought in the phone’s local app storage. This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it permanently. We cannot recover it for you.

2. Information handled by services we use

Cheeky Koko is built with the following third-party services. Each one collects information under its own privacy policy, linked below.

Google Play (purchases)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. Google handles your payment details; we never see or store your card or bank information. We receive confirmation of which item was bought, with a purchase reference, so the game can deliver it. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

Google Firebase (analytics, anonymous sign-in, app settings)

When the game first starts, Firebase creates a random, anonymous identifier for your installation. It is not linked to your name, email address or Google account. Firebase Analytics then collects:

  • the anonymous identifier and an app-instance identifier;
  • device model, Android version, app version, language, and your approximate country or region (worked out from your IP address);
  • game events: levels started, completed and failed; boosters used; rewarded ads watched; items bought; daily bonuses claimed; and character skin changes.

We use this to see which levels are too hard or too easy, find and fix problems, and understand which features people use. Firebase Remote Config lets the game check whether your version is still supported, so we can ask you to update if an old version stops working. See Firebase privacy and security.

Google AdMob (advertising)

The game shows ads, including optional ads you can watch for a reward. To choose, show and measure ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device information and how you interact with ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. Everywhere else, you can turn off ad personalisation or reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings (usually Settings › Privacy › Ads or Settings › Google › Ads). See How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Unity (game engine)

Cheeky Koko is made with the Unity engine. If the game crashes or runs into an engine fault, Unity may receive technical diagnostic data, such as device model, operating system and error details, to find and fix problems in the engine. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

4. What we do not collect

The game does not ask for or access your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location. You do not need an account to play.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for money. Information reaches the service providers above only so they can do the jobs described. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

6. How long information is kept

  • On your device: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Firebase Analytics: for a limited period set in our Firebase settings, and never longer than 14 months.
  • Google Play and AdMob: according to Google’s own retention policies, linked above.

7. Children

Cheeky Koko is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the game, contact us and we will delete what we can.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Stop all collection: uninstall the game.
  • Ads: change your consent choice in the game (EEA, UK and Switzerland), turn off ad personalisation, or reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.
  • Access or deletion: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to how it is used. Email us and we will respond within 30 days. Because the game has no accounts, the data held by these services is anonymous and usually cannot be traced back to you. We will delete whatever we can connect to your device.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for personalised ads, on our legitimate interest in improving and securing the game for analytics and diagnostics, and on the need to deliver what you paid for when handling purchases.

9. Security and international transfers

The services above send data over encrypted connections. Google and Unity may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States, with the safeguards described in their own policies.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game’s handling of information changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the game’s Google Play listing.
